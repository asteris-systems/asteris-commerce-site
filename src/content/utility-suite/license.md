# Licence Terms — Asteris Utility Suite

Asteris Utility Suite is GPL-2.0+ software. All tiers — Free, Starter, Pro, Agency — use the same open-source licence. The differences between tiers are commercial (site activation limits + support SLA), not legal.

---

## GPL-2.0+ licence

The Asteris Utility Suite plugin code is distributed under the GNU General Public License version 2.0 or later. This means:

- You can use the software on any site within your tier's activation limit
- You can modify the code for your own use
- If you distribute modified versions, you must do so under GPL-2.0+
- The licence does not restrict your commercial use of the software (you can use Asteris Utility Suite on a client's commercial site)

The GPL licence covers the plugin software. It does not cover access to **updates**, **priority support**, or **future modules** — those are commercial services attached to your active subscription.

---

## Site activation limits per tier

| Tier | Production-site activations |
|------|------:|
| Asteris Utility Suite Free (WordPress.org) | Unlimited (GPL — no licence-key restriction) |
| Asteris Starter | 1 |
| Asteris Pro | 3 |
| Asteris Agency | 10 |

**What counts as a site.** A production site is a publicly accessible domain where Asteris Utility Suite serves real traffic. A local development environment, a staging subdomain (e.g. `staging.yourdomain.com`), or a CI sandbox for the same project does NOT count as a separate activation — the licence applies to the production site and its parallel non-production environments.

**Multisite networks.** Asteris Utility Suite is not officially supported on WordPress Multisite at v1.0. Multisite support is on the v1.x roadmap. Using a single-site licence on a Multisite installation is outside the supported scope until then.

## Transferring a licence

You can move a licence to a different domain within your site-activation limit at no cost. Email support@asteriscommerce.com with the old and new domain. Processed within 1 business day.

Licences are **not transferable to a different person or business entity**. Selling, gifting, or transferring a paid licence to someone outside the original purchasing entity is a licence violation. We reserve the right to revoke licences found to have been transferred to third parties.

---

## When a subscription lapses

If you let your subscription lapse (cancel, or fail to pay a renewal):

- Asteris Utility Suite continues to function on your site (the GPL code doesn't disappear)
- You stop receiving plugin updates
- You stop receiving security patches
- You lose access to the customer download portal
- Your priority support access ends

To resume updates and support, resubscribe via the customer portal at the standard price for your tier.

## Developer + agency rights

As a developer using Asteris Utility Suite on client sites:

- **Asteris Utility Suite Free:** No restriction. GPL applies. Use it on any client site.
- **Asteris Starter (1 site):** Single-site use only.
- **Asteris Pro (3 sites):** Install on up to 3 production sites under one licence.
- **Asteris Agency (10 sites):** Install on up to 10 production sites you actively manage.
- **End-of-engagement transfer:** When you hand a site over to a client to self-manage, they need their own licence to continue receiving updates and support. The Agency tier doesn't extend to clients who self-manage the plugin after a build is complete — they would need their own tier of Asteris.
- **Reselling:** You cannot resell Asteris Utility Suite licences or bundle them into your own product offering. Bundle pricing for agencies is available — contact support@.
