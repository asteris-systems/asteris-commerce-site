# Running Asteris Free and a paid Asteris licence together

Asteris Utility Suite Free and a paid tier (Starter, Pro, or Agency) run together on the same WordPress install. You don't uninstall the free version to install a paid tier.

---

## How it works

Asteris Utility Suite is one plugin file. The licence key activates additional modules within the same plugin.

When you purchase a paid tier:

1. Download the updated plugin from your customer portal (it's the same plugin with the paid module code included)
2. Upload and activate, or let the in-place updater handle the swap if you already have the right version
3. Enter your licence key in **Asteris → Licence**
4. The 7 paid-only modules and full versions of the 5 lite modules appear in your module list immediately

---

## Which modules are active at each stage

| | Asteris Utility Suite Free | After paid licence |
|---|---|---|
| **Modules toggleable** | 5 lite | All 12 |
| **Licence required** | No | Yes |
| **Site limit** | Unlimited | Per tier (Starter 1 / Pro 3 / Agency 10) |
| **Updates** | Via WordPress.org plugin updater | Via the plugin's built-in updater (paid customers) |
| **Support** | WP.org forum + email (2-3 business days) | Email (2-3 business days Starter; 1 business day Pro / Agency) |

---

## What happens if the paid licence lapses

If your subscription isn't renewed:

- The 7 paid-only modules deactivate
- The full versions of the 5 lite modules revert to lite
- All settings and data are preserved in the database
- Resubscribing restores everything

**Resubscribing after a lapse re-enters you at the standard price** for your tier.
