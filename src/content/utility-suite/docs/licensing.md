# Asteris Utility Suite — Licensing

Asteris Utility Suite is GPL-2.0+ software. All tiers use the same open-source licence — the differences between tiers are commercial (site limits + support SLA), not legal.

For the full legal text, see [/license](/asteris-utility-suite/license/). This page covers practical questions.

---

## Site limits

| Tier | Production sites |
|------|----------------:|
| Asteris Utility Suite Free | Unlimited (GPL — no licence-key restriction) |
| Starter | 1 |
| Pro | 3 |
| Agency | 10 |

**One standard price.** Everyone pays the same standard price for each tier. Site limits follow the tier you subscribed at.

**Staging:** A staging subdomain of the same production site doesn't count as a separate site.

**Local development:** Local dev environments don't count as sites.

---

## Transferring a licence

Email support@asteriscommerce.com with your old domain and new domain. No fee. Processed within 1 business day.

Transfers must remain within the same buyer's ownership. Selling, gifting, or transferring a paid licence to a different person or business entity is a licence violation.

---

## What happens when a subscription lapses

The 7 paid-only modules and the full versions of the 5 lite modules deactivate. The 5 Free modules (lite tier) keep working. Settings are preserved — resubscribing restores everything.

**Resubscribing after a lapse re-enters you at the standard price** for your tier.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use my licence on a client's site?**
Pro (3 sites) and Agency (10 sites) allow client-site installs within their site limit. Starter (1 site) is for one site only. Agency doesn't extend to clients self-managing the plugin after delivery — they'd need their own tier.

**Can I resell Asteris Utility Suite licences?**
No. The licence is for your own use (or your agency's client builds within your tier limit), not for resale or bundling into your own product offering.

**Is the code available to modify?**
Yes. GPL-2.0+ permits modification. If you distribute modified versions, GPL terms apply.

**Is pricing the same for everyone?**
Yes. One standard price for everyone — Starter $149/yr, Pro $349/yr, Agency $549/yr. No founder or launch offers.
