# WPForms Alternative — Asteris vs WPForms Pro

**Looking for a WPForms alternative?** Asteris Utility Suite includes a Forms module with 20 field types, **conditional logic** (not Pro-gated), **multi-step forms** (not Pro-gated), 5 native integrations (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, MailerLite, Slack, Zapier), and three layers of anti-spam (honeypot + Cloudflare Turnstile + hCaptcha) — bundled with 10 other modules from $149/yr.

**Is there a free WPForms alternative with conditional logic?** Yes — Asteris Forms includes conditional logic in the paid tier ($149/yr Starter), which is cheaper than WPForms' Pro tier ($199/yr) and includes 10 other modules. For genuinely free forms with conditional logic, **Fluent Forms** is the best standalone option.

## When WPForms is the right answer

You need a specific high-end feature that's Pro+ in WPForms and not in Asteris yet — payment gateway integration, AI form builder, surveys with NPS, calculations, signature with witness fields. WPForms Pro has the most field-type depth in the WordPress forms market. If you need that depth, take it.

## When Asteris is the right answer

You need a competent, well-built forms module *plus everything else a WordPress site needs*. The Asteris Forms module covers the 80% of use cases (contact forms, newsletter signups, file uploads, multi-step) with the integrations most sites use — without the Pro-tier upsell wall. Conditional logic and multi-step shouldn't cost $200/yr extra; with Asteris they don't.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there a free WPForms alternative with conditional logic?**
Yes — **Fluent Forms** offers conditional logic in its free version (Asteris Forms is paid-only at the moment). For a paid alternative that's cheaper than WPForms Pro and includes 10 other modules, see Asteris Starter at $149/yr.

**Does Asteris Forms include Mailchimp and ConvertKit integrations?**
Yes — both, plus MailerLite, Slack, and Zapier webhooks. WPForms gates Mailchimp to the Plus tier and ConvertKit to Pro+.

**How many field types does Asteris Forms support?**
20 at v1.0 — text, email, phone, number, address, dropdown, radio, checkbox, file upload, signature, rating, date/time, conditional logic groups, multi-step page breaks, and more. WPForms Pro has ~30; Fluent Forms has 40+. For depth, WPForms Pro and Fluent Forms win; for parity at a bundled price, Asteris matches.

**Can I build multi-step forms without WPForms Pro?**
Yes — Asteris Forms supports multi-step out of the box (no Pro tier required at the paid Asteris level). Fluent Forms gates multi-step to its Pro tier.

**Can I import forms from WPForms to Asteris?**
A WPForms importer isn't in v1.0. Existing WPForms can stay on WPForms while new forms move to Asteris — they coexist without conflict.
